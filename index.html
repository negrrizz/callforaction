STUCK AT 28 · Give Baku’s Commuters a Way Forward.

BAKU SHOULD MOVE. NOT STAND STILL.

Thousands of commuters are facing crowded buses, longer journeys and higher transport costs. We believe there is a better way forward.

SIGN THE PETITIONSEE OUR SOLUTION

One Closed Connection. Thousands of Interrupted Journeys.

  • The metro connection between 28 May and Nizami was suspended on August 15, 2026.
  • Passengers now depend on replacement buses.
  • Bus capacity is significantly lower than metro train capacity.
  • Some commuters must pay three fares for a journey that previously required one.
  • People with disabilities, older passengers and people with lower incomes are particularly affected.

Three Moves. One Better Baku.

SPREAD THE RUSH

Flexible government working hours and 30–40% of university classes online, where quality can be maintained.

SPEED UP THE BUSES

A continuous, camera-enforced bus lane for M2 and M3, together with a free 90-minute transfer after a metro card tap.

MAKE IT WALKABLE

Safe pedestrian routes and accessible micromobility connections to metro stations.

A 30-Day Pilot. A Real Opportunity for Change.

WITHIN 30 DAYS

Introduce flexible government working hours.
Establish the continuous bus lane.

WITHIN 60 DAYS

Introduce the free 90-minute transfer.

BEFORE SPRING TERM

Establish the first safe pedestrian routes.

This is a pilot. It can be evaluated before any wider implementation, so the risk is small and the lessons are real.

Your Journey Matters. Make Your Voice Heard.

We call on the Land Transport Agency and Baku Metro to approve a 30-day pilot project for M2 and M3, introducing flexible working hours, a continuous bus lane, and a unified fare system.
